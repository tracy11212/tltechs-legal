TL Techs LLC — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

TL Techs LLC ("TL Techs," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you interact with us, including through our website, phone calls, text messages (SMS/MMS/RCS), Facebook and Messenger, and in-person service visits.

Information We Collect

We may collect: your name, phone number, email address, service address, and details about the HVAC/electrical service you request (e.g., equipment type, appointment details, photos of equipment for quotes/rebates).

How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule and provide HVAC, electrical, and related home-service work
  • To send appointment confirmations, service estimates/quotes, and job follow-ups
  • To respond to your inquiries and service requests
  • To send maintenance reminders and, where you've consented, promotional offers
  • To process rebates and manufacturer/utility incentive claims on your behalf, where applicable

SMS / MMS / RCS Messaging

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties for marketing purposes. By providing your mobile number, you consent to receive service-related and, where applicable, promotional text messages from TL Techs LLC. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages, or HELP for assistance. You may also contact us directly at 832-430-9042 or tltechsco@gmail.com.

Facebook and Messenger

When you message our Facebook Page, or comment on one of our posts and we follow up privately, Facebook shares with us your public profile name, a Page-scoped ID, and the messages and comments you send us. We use this information only to answer your question, send the estimate or information you asked for, and schedule service. Some replies are prepared by an automated assistant that answers on our behalf; a member of our team can take over the conversation at any time. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising outside of Facebook.

To have your Facebook and Messenger data deleted, follow our data deletion instructions.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our business (e.g., scheduling software, payment processing, messaging platforms), manufacturer/utility rebate administrators for claims you request, and as required by law.

Data Retention & Security

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide services, comply with legal/warranty obligations, and support rebate/warranty claims, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or opt out of marketing communications at any time, by contacting us at the information below. Use of our website and messaging channels is also subject to our Terms of Service.
