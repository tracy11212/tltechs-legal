TL Techs LLC — Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

You can ask TL Techs LLC to delete the information we received about you through Facebook and Messenger: your name, your Page-scoped ID, and the messages and comments you sent to our Page.

How to request deletion

Use any one of these:

  • Send the message "Delete my data" to the TL Techs A/C Facebook Page in Messenger.
  • Email tltechsco@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the name on your Facebook profile.
  • Call or text 832-430-9042.

What happens next

We confirm your request within 3 business days and delete your Facebook and Messenger data from our systems within 30 days. We keep only what the law requires us to keep, such as invoices and warranty records for work we performed at your home. Messages you sent remain in your own Messenger inbox, where you can delete them yourself.

Removing our app from your Facebook account

You can also remove any connection between your Facebook account and TL Techs in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.
